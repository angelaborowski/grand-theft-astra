## Acceptance requirements

For every character, compare a matched front-view render against the source: facial proportions, hairstyle, body build, outfit cut, colours, accessories and footwear. Obtain or construct side/back views while documenting unseen details. Preserve the reference identity during modeling and texturing. Generated views are proposals, not measured evidence.

Produce a textured mesh with a deforming skeleton, Idle/Walk/Run/Greet animations and a Blender source. Check elbows, shoulders, knees, foot contact and hair/clothing intersections in motion. Check the GLB in the actual game before replacing a placeholder. Record real polygon and texture budgets after mesh inspection.
